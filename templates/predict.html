Input Value Understanding

Normal Traffic

Low packet rate, balanced forward/backward packets, moderate flow duration, and stable bytes/sec usually indicate normal network activity.

Attack Traffic

Very high packets/sec, abnormal bytes/sec, sudden packet length changes, or unusual flow patterns may indicate DDoS or suspicious traffic.

Average / Medium Risk

Values between normal and abnormal ranges may produce medium severity. These cases should be monitored using history and recommendation.
